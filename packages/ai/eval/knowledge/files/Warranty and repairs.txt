Orbit Bikes - Warranty and repairs
Edition 2026, valid for bikes delivered from 1 January 2026.
1. What the warranty covers
The frame and the motor are covered for 2 years from the delivery date.
The battery is covered for 2 years or 500 charge cycles, whichever comes first.
A battery is replaced under warranty when its capacity falls below 70 percent
of its original capacity within that period.
The display, the charger and the lights are covered for 1 year.
Cosmetic damage such as paint scratches, dents and faded decals is not covered.
Wear parts - tyres, inner tubes, brake pads, chains and grips - are not covered.
---
2. What voids the warranty
The warranty does not apply when the bike has been modified: a replaced or
reprogrammed controller, a battery not sold by Orbit, or a derestricted motor.
Repairs by anyone other than an Orbit service partner void the warranty on the
part repaired.
Water damage from submersion, pressure washing or riding through deep water is
not covered, nor is damage from racing or jumping.
---
3. How to make a claim
Open the Orbit app, choose Support, then Warranty claim.
Attach photos of the problem and your frame number; the frame number is printed
under the bottom bracket.
We answer every claim within 3 business days.
If the claim is approved we arrange courier collection free of charge and
repair the bike within 15 business days of receiving it, or replace it.
We do not provide a loaner bike while yours is being repaired.
A rejected claim comes with a repair quote you can accept or decline.
