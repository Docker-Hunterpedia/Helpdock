9 · Notes
  • The strip is the one role="status" outside the thread. Its countdown is aria-hidden; what is read is the state and the closing time, so it speaks when the state changes, not every minute.
  • The thread is a log with aria-live="polite" and aria-relevant="additions": a new message, a reaction line or a failure strip is read; Sent, Delivered and Read changing in place are not. Nothing inside it is a live region of its own.
  • Each reply’s accessible name says its state (“Reply from Lina Haddad, read”). Delivered and Read come from the platform’s reports and never move back.
  • The window reads the conversation’s last customer message. A reaction, an edit, an echo or a delivery report does not reopen it.
  • Refused is drawn like DeliveryFailure with a ban icon and no Retry. Failed keeps Retry, which resumes after the last part the platform accepted.
  • Stickers are images, story media keeps its link until the story expires, and a quoted message shows as a leading quote line when it is on the ticket.
