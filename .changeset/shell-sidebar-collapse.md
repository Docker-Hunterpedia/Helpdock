Admin: the sidebar collapses to a 56 px icon rail and expands again, from the "Collapse sidebar" button or with ⌘\ / Ctrl+\. Each icon names itself in a tooltip, counts become badges, and Views opens in a popover. The browser remembers the choice; without one, the sidebar starts collapsed on windows between 1024 and 1279 px wide.
