HelpCenter/Portal-Tickets · M21-03, M21-05

  • Mine lists tickets the customer requested or is a participant of, on every channel. Each row: subject, reference, when it last changed, and one of four statuses: Open, Waiting for you, Waiting for us, Solved. No priority, tags, assignee or SLA.
  • Company (named after the account) appears only when the customer may see the account's tickets: every member when the account is set to “All members”, managers when it is set to “Managers”. Rows add “Opened by”. With “Own tickets”, or no account, the switch is not drawn.
  • The notice tells a member, on Mine, that coworkers can see what they open; a manager sees why they see everything. It is not dismissible: it states a privacy fact.
  • Only you marks a ticket staff made private to its requester; it never appears in Company for anyone else.
  • Open / Solved splits the list; Open is the default. Search matches subject and reference within what the customer may see. 20 rows, then Show more. Newest change first.
  • Empty says where tickets come from and offers New ticket. A ticket that is not yours answers 404 with the help center's not-found page, never “forbidden”.
  • Rendering. Server-rendered like the rest of the help center; a new reply shows on the next load. Pages are never cached.
  • Accessibility. The switches are links with aria-current; each row is one link whose name is the subject, the reference and the status; status is text, not colour alone.
