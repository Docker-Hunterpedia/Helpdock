Orbit stores and test rides

Flagship stores

  • Berlin — Torstraße 12, 10119 Berlin
  • Amsterdam — Prinsengracht 40, 1015 DX Amsterdam
  • Dubai — Dubai Mall, level 2, next to the fountain entrance

All stores are open from 10:00 to 19:00, Monday to Saturday, and closed on Sunday.

Test rides

Test rides are free and take about 30 minutes. Bring a photo ID; no booking is needed, but you can reserve a slot in the Orbit app to skip the queue. Every store also runs service appointments booked through the app.
