Admin/Login-Passkey

  • Amends Admin/Login. The passkey button sits after the “or” divider, before the sign-in link and provider buttons: outlined, full width, Lucide KeyRound.
  • The email input gets autocomplete="username webauthn" and the page starts conditional mediation on load, so the browser offers saved passkeys in the email field's autofill. Choosing one signs in directly.
  • Both paths are passwordless (UV required). A successful assertion opens the session with no second-factor step.
  • Waiting state replaces the form body while the browser prompt is open; Cancel aborts the ceremony and returns to the form.
  • Refusals share one message for unknown credential, bad signature, deactivated or no-brand account (invalid-credentials). No hint about which.
  • Cancelled (NotAllowedError from the browser) is a neutral notice, not an error, and does not count against rate limits.
  • Browser without WebAuthn: the button is disabled with the caption under it. On a plain-http install (methods().passkey = false) the button and the autofill are not drawn at all.
  • RP ID drift (passkeys-rp-changed): the warning replaces the button; the rest of the form works.
  • SSO enforced: the passkey button is hidden and conditional mediation is not started, like every other local method (Admin/Login-SSO).
  • Notices are the Banner component with role=status (neutral, warning) or role=alert (error), placed above the email field and focused after a failed ceremony.
  • Arabic: mirrored; the email input stays dir=ltr; browser and product names stay Latin.
