Refund timelines

Updated 12 Sep 20262 min read

Once we receive and inspect your return, we issue the refund to your original payment method. How long it takes to appear depends on who you paid with.

Payment method
After we issue the refund
Credit or debit card
3–5 business days
PayPal
Same day
Store credit
Immediately
Tip. The refund email includes the date we issued it. Count business days from that date, not from the day you shipped the return.

If it has been longer than that

Check with your bank first, then contact us with your order number. We can send you the refund reference to pass on to them.
