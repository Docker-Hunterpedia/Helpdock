HelpCenter/Portal-SignIn · M21-02

  • Where. /sign-in on the brand's help center host (or under /hc/<brand>). The header gains My tickets and Sign in when the brand has the portal on; with it off neither shows and these addresses answer 404.
  • One answer. Submitting any well-formed address shows “Check your email” with the same words and timing whether or not it is a customer. Only a malformed address gets a field error. Over the limit (5 per address, 20 per IP, per hour) the form shows the warning and sends nothing.
  • The link opens “Continue to your tickets”. Only the Continue button (a POST) signs in; opening the page spends nothing, so mail scanners cannot use the link up. Expired and used links get their own pages with a way forward.
  • After Continue the customer lands on My tickets, or on the ticket they came for when they followed “View this ticket” from an email.
  • Signed in. The header shows the address with a menu: who and which company, Sign out, Sign out everywhere. Sessions last 30 days from the last visit.
  • The email uses the brand's customer layout and language of the page it was requested from. It names the address, says the link works once for 15 minutes, and says what to do if it was not asked for. No ticket content.
  • CAPTCHA. When the brand has the built-in check on for its web form, the same check sits above the button (as on WebForm); not drawn here.
  • Accessibility. One h1 per page, the field labelled and described by its error, errors announced, focus on the h1 after each step, targets at least 44 px, 16 px text, both directions.
