Admin/Account-Notifications-Schedule (+ Sounds)

  • Your account › Notifications gains, above the existing PreferenceMatrix, the Email schedule card: four RadioCards (Right away default, D-25), the digest hour and the person's time zone, Pause, and Send me a test email.
  • A status line heads the card (and the bell's panel) whenever something stops email: paused until a time with Resume now; unsubscribed on a date with Turn email back on; Email is off.
  • Pause menu: 1 hour, until tomorrow 09:00 in the person's time zone, 1 week, until a date. It holds email and push; the bell keeps everything.
  • Urgent kinds carry an “Urgent” caption in the matrix.
  • Admin/Account-Notifications-Sounds (M14-25): one Switch per event, all off by default, a preview button each, the waiting-chat reminder, and the note about background tabs. Nothing depends on hearing.
