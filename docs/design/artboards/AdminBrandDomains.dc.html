How a domain goes live
  1. Add it here.
  2. Create the CNAME and TXT records.
  3. Helpdock sees both and marks it verified.
  4. The certificate is issued on the first visit, unless Cloudflare proxies it.
States
Verified: DNS checks out and HTTPS works. Waiting: a record is missing or not seen yet. Failed: the reason is shown under the domain.
