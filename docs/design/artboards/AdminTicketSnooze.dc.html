Admin/Ticket-Snooze

  • Snooze is a status (D-20): a seeded “Snoozed” status in the on-hold hue. Entry points: a Snooze button in the ticket header, h, the palette, and the bulk bar.
  • Menu: four presets with their absolute time on the right; when the ticket's department works in another time zone, a caption gives both. “Pick a date and time…” opens the picker dialog (5 minutes to 90 days ahead).
  • On the ticket: the SnoozedStrip above the thread names who snoozed it, until when, and what wakes it early, with “Wake now”.
  • In lists: snoozed tickets leave My open, Unassigned, Overdue and saved views; the row shows a Clock and the wake time where the SLA timer was. A built-in Snoozed view sits under My open.
  • Waking by time or SLA sends a “snooze ended” notification. The resolution clock pauses; response clocks keep running (D-19).
  • Arabic: mirrored; times in the app's locale; time-zone names stay Latin.
