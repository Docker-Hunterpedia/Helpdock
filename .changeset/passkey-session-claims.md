Passkey ceremonies read the browser session through one helper that verifies the bearer token's signature and expiry and compares its subject with the guard's principal, throwing on anything less, so no claim an unverified token carried takes part in the comparison; the verification also runs unconditionally, which is the structure CodeQL's user-controlled-bypass alert 49 could not follow past. No behaviour change: a token claiming the caller but signed by another key, or not signed at all, was refused and still is.
