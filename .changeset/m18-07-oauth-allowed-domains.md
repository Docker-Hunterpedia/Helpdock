Google sign-in can be limited to your Google Workspace domains: list them under Settings › Authentication › Allowed Workspace domains (or pin `HD_OAUTH_GOOGLE_ALLOWED_DOMAINS='["example.com"]'`). With a list, only Workspace accounts on those domains sign in, connect or accept an invitation, accounts linked before included (open sessions are not ended), and everything else, Gmail included, sees "This Google account can't sign in here" (`oauth-domain-not-allowed`). An empty list keeps today's behaviour. An invitation can now be accepted with Google or GitHub instead of a password, when the provider account's verified address is the invited one. The security page's card is called Sign-in methods and shows when each provider last signed you in, a provider account that is not connected yet lands on a screen listing the steps to connect it, and every security email says when the change happened and, for a provider, which provider account it was (M18-07). Migration `0059_oauth_identity_use`.
