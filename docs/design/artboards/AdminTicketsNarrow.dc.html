Admin/Tickets-Narrow

  • Below 1024 px (LIST_BREAKPOINT) the list fills the screen; the sidebar is behind the menu button.
  • Opening a ticket pushes the ticket view with a back button naming the view; the arrow mirrors in RTL. Browser back returns to the list at the same scroll position.
  • Details open as a drawer from the inline end with a scrim; Esc and the scrim close it.
  • Selection, the bulk bar and the keyboard work the same. Phone layouts below 600 px are M17-07.
