HelpCenter/Portal-Ticket and Portal-NewTicket · M21-03, M21-04, M21-05

  • The page shows the subject, reference, status, who opened it, when and by which channel, then every public message oldest first with attachments. Staff appear as the brand shows an agent to a customer (DOMAIN-RULES §2.10). Internal notes, activity, tags, priority and SLA are never here.
  • Reply is a plain text box with attachments (the brand's content policy sets types and sizes). Sending stores a public message from the customer on the portal channel and returns to the page with it at the end and focus on it. An empty message gets a field error; a failed send keeps the text.
  • Mark as solved shows on the customer's own ticket while it is not closed; it asks nothing and can be undone by replying.
  • Solved replaces the reply box with the solved card: reply to reopen while the brand's reopen policy allows, and the rating when the brand asks for one. Closed past that window offers a new ticket that staff see linked to this one.
  • A coworker's ticket (company view) is labelled with who opened it; the reply box says whose ticket it is and that the name will be seen. No Mark as solved. Replying adds the coworker as a participant.
  • New ticket uses the brand's web form topics. The customer's address is known, so there is no email field. “Only I should see this ticket” shows only to a member whose company can see tickets; it sets the private mark.
  • Attachments open through short-lived authorised links; a link copied to someone else stops working.
  • Accessibility. Each message is an article named by its sender and time; the status is text; errors are tied to their fields; after sending, focus moves to the new message; 44 px targets; both directions.
