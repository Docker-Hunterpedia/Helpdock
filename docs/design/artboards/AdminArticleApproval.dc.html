Customs and VAT on clothing shipped to Germany

Clothing sent to Germany with a declared value under €150 is not charged customs duty. German import VAT of 19 % is still due and is collected by the carrier on delivery.

Who handles clearance #who-handles-clearance

We clear every EU parcel on your behalf. You do not need to file anything; keep the delivery receipt in case the carrier asks for it.

Paying the VAT at the door is normal. It is not an extra fee from us.

Parcels over €150 #parcels-over-150

Duty depends on the item type. The carrier sends you the amount by email before delivery.
