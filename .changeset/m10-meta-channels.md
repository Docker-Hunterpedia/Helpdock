WhatsApp numbers, Facebook Pages and Instagram professional accounts are ticket channels (M10, the Meta channels guide). Admins connect an account per platform on Channels › Meta by pasting its token, test it before saving, see its health and reconnect it when Meta drops the token or the subscription. A customer's message opens or continues a ticket. An agent's reply goes back on the same platform inside Meta's reply window, and a reply outside the window is refused when it is written. Migrations `0050_meta_channels` and `0051_meta_profile_checked` add the Meta tables and the three channel kinds. WhatsApp templates and the Human Agent tag come in a later release.
