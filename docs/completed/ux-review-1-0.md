# UX and UI review of 1.0.0

Status: review done; group A being fixed for 1.0.1, groups B and C planned in M14 and M19
Reviewed: 2026-10-20, against 1.0.0
Scope: the admin app, the widget and the help center, in `en` and `ar`

A read-only review of how 1.0.0 looks and behaves for agents, admins and customers, recorded like the [accessibility audit](accessibility-audit.md): what was looked at, what was found, and where each finding is being dealt with. Nothing was changed in the repository during the review and no test suite was run. The fixes are tracked by the `Status` column below; the `1.0.1 PR` column is filled in as the patch pull requests merge.

## Method

- **Admin app:** the 18 Playwright baselines in `apps/admin/e2e/__screenshots__/{en,ar}/` (1280 × 720, rendered against the fixtures in `apps/admin/src/auth/mock-api.ts`, not the real api), plus a reading of the source.
- **Design intent:** the artboard renders in [`docs/design/screens/`](../design/README.md) and [DESIGN.md](../../DESIGN.md).
- **Public pages:** screenshots of a local 1.0.0 demo taken with headless Chromium: the help center in both languages at 1440 and 390 px wide, its search, the web form, and the widget open on desktop and phone. These screenshots are not committed; a finding that rests on one says "demo screenshot". Signed-in screens of the demo were not reviewed.
- **Prior art:** other helpdesks' agent ergonomics (command palettes, shortcut sheets, bulk edit, snooze, setup checklists), including OpenDesk's, were used as a reference for what agents expect.

Severity: **High** hurts daily use or correctness for many users. **Medium** is a visible defect or friction on a main flow. **Low** is polish. Effort: **S** up to 1 day, **M** 2–4 days, **L** a week or more. A finding marked *Opinion* is a judgement call; every other finding points at a screenshot or a line of code.

## Summary

No redesign is needed: the visual system is coherent and calm, and the help center is polished. The findings sit in four clusters:

1. **The agent's main screen is cramped at common laptop sizes.** At 1280 px the four-column workspace leaves the thread about 400 px, and the always-expanded email composer leaves almost nothing of the conversation visible at 720 px tall (UX-01).
2. **RTL correctness has gaps in user content.** Subjects and message bodies have no `dir="auto"`, some identifiers are not isolated, and a few dates follow the browser locale (UX-02 to UX-04). The rules are already in DESIGN §7 and §10; most fixes are one attribute.
3. **What 1.0 already does is hard to discover.** Shortcuts exist but are never shown, there is no setup checklist after the wizard, empty states have no action, and the admin navigation does not scale (UX-09 to UX-11).
4. **Feedback while loading is thin.** No skeletons on the ticket list or ticket view (UX-12).

The review proposed three groups: **A**, small fixes that need no artboard, shipped as the 1.0.1 patch; **B**, agent workspace and first-run work with artboards first, planned in [M14 Inbox productivity](../in-development/M14-inbox-productivity.md); **C**, customer-surface work, planned in [M19 Customer surfaces polish](../in-development/M19-customer-surfaces-polish.md). One AI finding is covered by [M12 AI workspace](../in-development/M12-ai-workspace.md).

## Strengths to keep

The review also recorded what 1.0.0 does well, so later work does not lose it:

- **Arabic is first-class in the agent app.** Sign-in, TOTP, contacts and settings mirror cleanly, and IBM Plex Sans Arabic pairs well with Plex Sans in mixed lines. UX-02 to UX-04 protect this.
- **AI trust cues.** AI is named in words plus an AI badge, citations carry Public and Internal marks, "Show redacted" displays what the model received, each AI message carries its log (model, tokens, cost, confidence against the threshold), the ticket has an "AI on this ticket" card, and the budget meter has a hard stop. The widget offers "Talk to a human" with clear "stepped back" copy.
- **Help center quality.** Rendered by the server and readable without scripts, with a skip link, bilingual search results that keep their own `lang` and `dir`, highlighted terms, topic filters with counts, and a no-result state with topic chips and "Still need help?".
- **Widget accessibility discipline.** 44 px targets, log semantics without nested live regions, message states that never rely on colour alone, focus trapping on phones only, and a send icon mirrored in RTL.
- **Collision and SLA visibility.** Viewing and replying indicators, SLA chips in list rows, and an SLA card with business-time readings.
- **Honest settings.** Fields locked by the environment name their variable, Save and Discard stay disabled until something changes, and secrets are never shown again.
- **Contact identity.** Verified and unverified per identity, a possible-duplicate review with "Not the same", and an explicit "hidden in other departments" line instead of silently missing tickets.
- **The design-first process.** Every screen has an artboard and DESIGN.md is precise; most findings are places where the code drifted from DESIGN.md, not gaps in it.
