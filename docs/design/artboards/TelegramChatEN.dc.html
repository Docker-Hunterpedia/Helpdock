How the bot’s messages look in a Telegram chat

A neutral frame, not Telegram’s own look. The customer’s messages sit at the inline end, the bot’s at the inline start. Everything the bot sends is plain text with inline buttons; no Markdown, so names and references are never parsed.

  1. 1
    Language prompt (M6-04)Sent on /start when the bot has a language prompt. Text from Channels › Telegram; the two buttons are fixed and always name each language in its own script. A tap records the contact’s language, then the bot edits the prompt to “Language: English” and drops the buttons.
  2. 2
    Welcome (M6-04)The welcome in the chosen language. Empty in the admin means no welcome. Neither message opens a ticket.
  3. 3
    Ticket opened (M6-02)Sent once, when a message opens a new ticket for this chat. Later messages join the open ticket silently. Never counts as a first response for the SLA.
  4. 4
    Agent reply (M6-02)A public reply goes out through the outbox as the bot. The first line names the agent by first name and the brand, since a bot has a single name. Attachments follow as photos or documents.
  5. 5
    Satisfaction survey (M8-06)Sent on close when the brand has CSAT on, unless the ticket is spam or merged. Five buttons record the rating in one tap; Add a comment opens the CSAT page with the score already chosen. The link works once and expires in 30 days.
  6. 6
    After a ratingThe bot replaces the buttons with a thanks line. A second tap, or a tap after 30 days, gets “This survey has closed.” and records nothing.
