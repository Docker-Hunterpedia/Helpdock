Keyboard shortcuts across the admin (M14-01): press `?` for the shortcut sheet, `g` then a letter to open a view or page, `e` to close the open ticket, `a`, `s`, `p` and `t` for its assignee, status, priority and tags, and `⌘`/`Ctrl`+`Enter` to send from the composer (add `Shift` to send and close). Shortcuts work on an Arabic keyboard layout, and a "Single-key shortcuts" switch in the sheet, saved to your account, turns off every shortcut without a modifier (WCAG 2.1.4).
