The widget's handoff line says when the team is back. After "Talk to a human" or the assistant's own handoff, a visitor writing out of hours reads "The team is away until Monday at 09:00 (Arabian Standard Time)" and that the message is saved, by the calendar of the conversation's department, else the brand's. A conversation and its `conversation` frame carry the new optional `hours` (`open`, `nextOpenAt`, `timezone`); clients built before it still work.
