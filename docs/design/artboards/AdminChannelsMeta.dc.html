How messages arrive
Meta posts every message for every account to this install’s one callback URL and signs it with the app secret; a request without a valid signature is refused. Helpdock files the text at once and fetches photos, voice notes and files in the background. An account answers for one brand only. Messenger and Instagram stop posting to an app whose address fails for an hour: Helpdock checks each subscription every hour and renews it, but the messages of the gap are not fetched again.
Health
Healthy: the token works and Meta sends this account’s messages to Helpdock. Expiring: an Instagram token Helpdock could not renew; it stops on the date shown. Failing: the token expired or was revoked, or the subscription could not be renewed. Waiting: connected, no message received yet.
Checked every hour by the health job; “Fix” opens the account’s page.
