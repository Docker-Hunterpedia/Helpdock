Admin/Composer-Shortcuts (+ Admin/Composer-Mentions)

  • /refund completes inline (the rest in faint text) and Tab or Enter inserts it. / at the start of a line or after a space opens the picker, ordered by your recent use, then the brand's; a / inside a URL is left alone.
  • {{ opens the existing PlaceholderPicker with sample values.
  • Saved-reply and macro editors gain a Shortcut field; save refuses unknown placeholders or an unclosed {{, naming each. Fallbacks like {{contact.first_name|there}} are allowed.
  • Mentions: @ in a note (never a public reply) opens a listbox of people who can see this ticket's department, with presence, filtered in either script. Choosing inserts the handle.
