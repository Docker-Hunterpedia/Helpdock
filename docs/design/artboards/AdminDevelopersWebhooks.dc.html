When a delivery fails

Any answer other than 2xx, a timeout after 30 s, a redirect or a refused address counts as a failure. Helpdock tries again up to 8 attempts in all:
  1. 1now
  2. 2+1 min
  3. 3+5 min
  4. 4+30 min
  5. 5+2 h
  6. 6+6 h
  7. 7+12 h
  8. 8+24 h
After the eighth, the delivery is marked failed and can be replayed by hand. Replay sends the same event id, so a receiver that stores ids sees it once.
