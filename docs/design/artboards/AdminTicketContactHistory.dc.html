Previous tickets on the contact card

M1-04 follow-up. The DetailsPanel's contact card gains a Previous tickets section so a returning customer is visible without leaving the ticket.

  • Source: the contact timeline the panel already reads (department-scoped, DOMAIN-RULES §1.2). The open ticket itself is left out.
  • Heading caption 12/500 text.secondary with the count in mono: tickets this viewer may see, excluding this one.
  • Up to three rows, most recent first: subject 13/500 (ellipsis) over a caption with the mono reference in bdi and the created date; StatusBadge at the inline end. A row is one link to that ticket, ≥ 44 px, hover bg.muted.
  • Under the list: the existing “N hidden (other departments)” line in status.warning.text when any are hidden, then a link to the contact page, named after the contact.
  • No previous visible tickets: the heading and “This is their first ticket.” When only hidden ones exist, that sentence is replaced by the hidden line.
  • No contact on the ticket: the section is not drawn. Loading: three 44 px Skeleton rows.
  • Arabic: the panel mirrors; subjects keep their own language and direction (lang, dir="auto"); references and addresses in bdi.
