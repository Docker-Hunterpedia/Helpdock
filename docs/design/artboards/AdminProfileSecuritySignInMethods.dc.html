Admin/Profile-Security-SignInMethods

  • Supersedes Admin/Me-SignInProviders (1.0.2): renamed “Sign-in methods”, gains Microsoft, and the step-up dialog gains “Use your passkey”.
  • Row order follows the sign-in page: Google, Microsoft, GitHub. Unconfigured providers are not listed; with none configured the card is not drawn.
  • The linked account's email is in bdi dir="ltr" (the email_at_link value, which may differ from the Helpdock email).
  • Disconnect is refused when it would leave no first factor (no password, no passkey, no other provider): disabled with the caption saying what to add first. A passkey counts as a first factor.
  • The 1.0.1 notice from Admin/Me-SignInProviders keeps its rule (shown only while nothing is linked).
  • Connect returns here with a success toast, or to the callback screens in Admin/Login-Providers.
