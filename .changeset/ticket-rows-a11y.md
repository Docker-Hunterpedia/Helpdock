**Ticket rows a screen reader can triage, and skeletons while the desk loads.** A row in the ticket list used to be announced as its reference and subject alone; it is now named by everything it shows: the priority (said in words, since the dot is only colour), subject, reference, contact, assistant state, status and the SLA as a sentence ("4h left"). The ticket list draws skeleton rows until its first page arrives, and an opening ticket draws the shape of its header and messages instead of a blank panel.
